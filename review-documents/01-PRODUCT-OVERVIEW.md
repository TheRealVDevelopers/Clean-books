# Product Overview

## What is CleanBook?

CleanBook is a two-sided business automation and compliance platform designed for Chartered Accountants, auditors, accountants and tax consultants serving multiple SME clients.

It connects:

1. A **practice dashboard** used by the CA or auditor.
2. A **custom business application** used by each referred client.

The objective is to make the client's daily operational data automatically useful for bookkeeping, GST, tax and audit work.

## Problem being solved

CA firms commonly receive incomplete information through WhatsApp, spreadsheets, paper bills and late document uploads. Staff spend significant time chasing clients, cleaning data and rebuilding records before filing.

Business owners also lack a simple, reliable view of what is happening across sales, employees, vehicles, inventory, receivables and compliance.

CleanBook reduces this gap by capturing information at the point where business activity happens.

## Core product logic

1. A CA or auditor introduces a business client.
2. The Real V Developers studies that client's operations.
3. A customized automation application is built for the business.
4. The owner and employees use the application for daily work.
5. Relevant operational and financial records flow to CleanBook.
6. The CA receives organized, review-ready information instead of chasing raw documents.
7. The proposed commercial model includes a recurring referral share for the introducing practice, subject to legal and ICAI review.

## Main users

- **CA principal or auditor:** portfolio overview, risk, approvals, filings and revenue.
- **CA staff:** assigned clients, reconciliations, document requests and tasks.
- **Business owner:** simple view of operations, people, money and compliance requests.
- **Business manager:** operational screens permitted by role.
- **Employee or driver:** restricted attendance, trip, upload or task actions.
- **Platform administrator:** firm onboarding, integrations, billing and support.

## Product differentiation

CleanBook is not intended to be only a generic accounting dashboard. Each client's operating application differs by business type while sharing a common compliance and accounting data layer.

Examples:

- Transport: fleet, drivers, trips, PODs, vehicle documents and E-Way Bills.
- Gym: members, renewals, dues and trainer schedules.
- Restaurant: orders, inventory, wastage and aggregator settlements.
- Manufacturing: job cards, production stages, dispatch and machine downtime.
- Contractor: sites, labour, materials, milestones and RA bills.

## Current prototype outcome

The prototype demonstrates how a reviewer can move between the CA's practice dashboard and a client's custom application, perform demo actions, and see how structured client activity can support accounting and compliance work.
