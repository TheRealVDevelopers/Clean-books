## Current status

CleanBook is a functional front-end prototype with realistic demo data. It demonstrates the complete product concept but is not yet a production accounting, GST filing or government-portal system.

## Important reviewer note

- All financial, client and filing records are fictional demo data.
- Actions are stored in browser session state and are not permanently saved.
- Firebase initialization is prepared, but Firebase is not connected to application workflows yet.
- GST, e-Invoice, E-Way Bill and return filing are not submitted to government portals.
- AI responses are currently rule-based demonstrations, not a connected AI service.
