# CleanBook brand system

## Positioning

**CleanBook is the operating workspace that helps CA firms see what is due,
what is missing and what happens next — without chasing clients across WhatsApp,
Excel and paper files.**

It is not presented as accounting software or as a replacement for CA judgement.
The future AI layer prepares, explains and flags; a CA reviews every decision.

## Logo

The mark is an open ledger shaped like a **C**, with a check inside it.

- The open C = work that becomes visible rather than buried.
- The check = a clear, completed next step.
- The amber dot = an item requiring human attention.

Use `src/assets/cleanbook-mark.svg` for an app icon or social avatar. Use
`src/assets/cleanbook-lockup.svg` on light product materials. Never stretch,
recolour or add a drop shadow to either mark.

## Typography

- **Inter**: all interface, product and social copy.
- **JetBrains Mono / system monospace**: numbers, dates, GSTINs, filing IDs and
  money figures only.
- No condensed thumbnail fonts, gradients, purple neon or finance-stock-chart
  clichés. CleanBook should feel calm, precise and audit-ready.

## Voice

Plain, specific and calm. Prefer: “What is missing?”, “Ready to review”,
“Waiting on client”, “Clear next step.” Avoid: “revolutionary”, “fully
automated”, “AI does your compliance”, or any claim that suggests filings occur
without CA approval.

## Public launch language

**Use:** “We’re building an AI-assisted operating workspace for CA firms.”

**Do not use yet:** “AI-integrated accounting software”, “automated filing”,
or performance claims. Phase 3 AI is planned; the current assistant preview is
rule-based demo functionality.
